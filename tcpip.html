Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol

TCP/IP is a set of communication protocols used to connect devices on the internet and ensure reliable data transmission.

TCP/IP works as a two-part system:

  • IP (Internet Protocol): Responsible for addressing and routing data packets so they reach the correct destination.
  • TCP (Transmission Control Protocol): Ensures that the data arrives correctly, in order, and without errors.

Data sent over the internet is broken into small packets. These packets may take different routes but are reassembled correctly at the destination using TCP.
